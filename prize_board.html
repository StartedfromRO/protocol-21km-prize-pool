General Terms & Eligibility

1. Cash prizes will be remitted to the winners' verified bank accounts within 30-45 days post-event after timing chips are validated and medical/doping clearances are cleared. 2. A physical trophy will be awarded to each individual podium placer (1st to 5th) and the top 3 teams in the Group Relay. 3. For Open categories, a maximum of two non-Malaysian participants are eligible to place inside the top 5 cash prize positions.
